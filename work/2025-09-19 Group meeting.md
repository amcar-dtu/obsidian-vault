It is actually sub-poissonian: variance << mean

From Fox:

$$ g^2(0) = \dfrac{\left\langle a^\dagger a (a^\dagger a - 1)\right\rangle}{\left\langle a^\dagger a \right\rangle^2} = 1 + \dfrac{\left\langle (\Delta \hat{n})^2\right\rangle - \left\langle \hat{n}\right\rangle}{\left\langle \hat{n}\right\rangle^2} = 0 $$
Here one can see that this relation is valid only if the variance $\left\langle (\Delta \hat{n})^2\right\rangle$ goes to 0 and the average $\left\langle \hat{n}\right\rangle$ goes to 1.
